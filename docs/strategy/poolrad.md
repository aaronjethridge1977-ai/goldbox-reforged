# Pool of Radiance strategy

Pool of Radiance's route, hard fights, Pool-only rules and treasure, for players. General tactics are in the Gold Box
Players' Guide (`docs/playbook.md`); its tags (*seen*, *code*, *book*, *players*, *Jeremy*, **unconfirmed**) apply
here. Rewritten by Gold Box Guide 1 (2026-11-20) with Jeremy, section by section: sections 1-6 are done (section 3 in
part; the rest of the old hard-fight notes are parked below for review). New lessons go in the Inbox at the end; the
Lead folds them in daily.

## 1. The party

- **The guide's example party** (Jeremy, 2026-11-20): human fighter, dwarf fighter/thief, two human clerics, elf
  fighter/magic-user, human magic-user (guide section 1).
- **The comparison party** the Player lane uses for the Stage 1 match: ARN (fighter), BRAM (fighter), CEDRIC (cleric),
  ELWEN (cleric/fighter/magic-user), LIRA (fighter/magic-user), DORN (fighter/thief); the NPC DIRTEN in early fights.
  Its levels below are what it had, not what we recommend.
- **The cluebook's one marker:** a party that can clear the Slums' troll-and-ogre room can handle the rest. *book*
- **Races:** humans can't multi-class in Pool, so let humans be the single-class characters and demi-humans the
  multi-classes; a half-elf cleric stops at level 5. *book* (Cluebook, Manual) + *Jeremy*

Stojanow Gate and Valjevo Castle last. *players* ([GameBanshee](https://gamebanshee.com/poolofradiance/walkthrough/gameplaytips.php))

**Notes by stretch** (*seen* unless marked; the hard fights are in section 3)
1. **New Phlan:** buy, ready everything, rest at the inn, memorize.
2. **The Slums:** heal after each event; avoid the southern streets (6,14) at level 1; an inn trip between visits; the
   trolls and ogres last (section 3).
3. **Sokal Keep:** GIVE Ohlo the potion, see the harbor master, take the boat from the pier's end. Event 1: TALK, NICE.
   The pale form: PARLAY. Ferran Martinez: parley, say LUX, tell the truth (*book*). The big battle (event 9) can wait
   until level 3 (section 3).
4. **Kuto's Well:** CLIMB DOWN NO, NAILED SHUT YES; in the catacombs LOOK for the secret door (5,8 N); Norris: FIGHT.
5. **Mendor's Library:** only a member with a free hand can ready a brass mirror; the basilisk: COMBAT, raise saves,
   kill it before it gazes (*book*); the library spectre attacks if you took books (*book*).
6. **City Hall, Podol:** the clerk: from 4,5 step east, not north. Podol: DISGUISE, the auction, the buccaneer duel.
7. **Textile House:** five trips by band and boat; the graveyard commission brings the council's Two-Handed Sword +1
   (+3 vs undead) and 4 Restoration scrolls; in 4:2 use cures only and rest at the inn; the chained man: FREE x3, then
   LEAVE.
8. **Nomads, Bane:** the feast, lodging, the kobold waves (STAY, DESTROY), the shaman's reward. Bane: goblin slaves
   KILL; hay beds SEARCH; the trap door at 10,9 needs a find roll.
9. **Kovel Mansion:** traps AVOID (or clear them if the loot is worth it); locks PICK once, then BREAK.
10. **The kobold caves:** enter by the large wyvern cave and rest there; free Princess Fatima (she joins); tell the
    efreeti the truth (*book*). The throne room: section 3.
11. **Sorcerer's Island and the pyramid:** round the poisoned river's west side to the rowboat (6,14); password NOKNOK;
    the fountain: MOVE QUICKLY AWAY; the dial on BLUE; MOVE ON at the rocks; cures only in 7:22; the ruined castle is
    anti-magic (heal by resting). Parley Nice with the caged lizard men (*book*).
12. **Buccaneers, Zhentil:** PUNCH MERCHANT, RELEASE ANIMALS, the pen gate (PICK, BASH), GET HIM; the captain:
    SURRENDER; envoys YES; no camp in 6:28.
13. **The graveyard:** not before the council's sword and the Restoration scrolls; nine trips; CURE DISEASE after the
    mummies; the tower door (15,6): LEAVE (section 3).
14. **Stojanow Gate:** buy the merchant's wagon by day (section 3).
15. **Valjevo Castle:** section 3 and section 4's rules.

## 3. Hard fights

First pass (2026-11-20): Jeremy reviewed the Slums, Sokal Keep, the Textile House and the throne room; the rest is
parked until he circles back. Enemies are the game's own (fight scripts: docs/cover-fights.md; names: the MON*CHA.DAX
files); results *seen* unless marked.

**The Slums (2:20)**
- **The southern streets (6,14):** a wandering encounter of 24, either 4 goblin leaders, a bugbear and 19 goblin
  guards, or 4 orc leaders, a bugbear and 19 orcs. *code* It destroyed two level-1 parties (p14b3, p14b4) and killed a
  member in two others (p14b5, p15c3). **Two Sleeps should cut it down** (*Jeremy*): Sleep's 4d4 hit dice cover goblins and orcs
  (1 each), but its 3x3 holds at most 9 figures, so bunch them first (a corner or a doorway), then Sleep.
- **Event 9 (3,3):** 4 orc leaders and 20 orcs (24 in one load; the Player counted 30: perhaps a second wave). *code* +
  *seen* Lost twice at level 1 by a party that had spent its Sleeps on the way (p15c1, c2). Two fresh Sleeps should do
  much of the work. *Jeremy*
- **The trolls and ogres (0,14):** 2 ogres and 4 trolls; the cluebook's test of the party. *code*
  - Works: two low-AC fighters hold the ogres in the narrow room so they block the trolls; everyone else shoots and
    casts at the trolls from one square back; drop the trolls together and stand on a fallen troll's square so it can't
    rise (its corner, top-left square, and the MOVE ended with Enter: *seen*, Player 40); then the ogres. *book* Cast Sleep at the ogres twice: if it fails, little is lost. *Jeremy* (2026-11-20) In the original, on this
    room: 10 Sleeps (4d4 = 7-13) put 2 ogres down and never a troll, about 1 cast in 5, so two casts give roughly a 1 in 3
    chance of at least one sleeping ogre (small sample: anywhere from about 1 in 10 to 3 in 4), and a sleeping ogre is
    slain outright. Keep the party out of the 3x3 (the caster too). *seen* (Combat Chat 34)
  - Results: at level 3, won deathless at the second try (p16e2); from an earlier save, won 2 of 2, both with deaths
    (Player 8).
- From the cluebook (merged by Gold Box Guide 8, Jeremy 09:29): Sleep the goblin guards' leaders. *book*

**The finale (5:7)** (Gold Box Guide 3 with Jeremy, 2026-11-20 17:37)
- **Genheeris** (the robed man at the desk, 2,5): agree to his deal and he **joins** (a 7th-level magic-user, 22 HP,
  carrying a **Wand of Lightning Bolt with 56 charges** (MON5ITM 88: byte 60; the 10 is its plus): the finale's wand),
  unless the party already has 8. *code* (ECL5 block 7, NPC_ADD 88; charges Gold Box Guide 4)
- **The 12 guards (3,8):** 8th-level fighters (87 HP each, about 1,044 in all; 1,370 experience each); no PARLAY;
  **FLEE gets away** with the party's slowest at movement 9 (to 3,6; come back later). They are human: **Hold Person and
  Stinking Cloud take them**, and a held guard dies to one blow (a fighter 8 fails the save about 60%; a cast picks up to
  three, about 1.8 held; CEDRIC's and ELWEN's 2nd-level slots and the magic-user Hold Person, about 6 casts, hold about
  10 of 12); the wands and the clouds for the rest; Fireball hardly matters against 87 HP. *code* + *Jeremy*
- **Tyranthraxus follows at once, no camp between** (80 HP, AC 0, THAC0 5, two attacks, a lightning breath, fear). In
  the original he resists every spell (the original's bug #7): only blows count. So **Haste, Prayer and Bless are cast
  in the guards' last rounds**, once most are held, to be fresh for him (Haste lasts 3 + level rounds: cast in camp
  before the guards it may run out). **An effect carries into the next fight with the rounds it has left** (a fight's
  end clears nothing; Combat Chat 36, *code*), and **5:7's script passes no time between the two fights** (ECL5 block 7,
  0xA614 to 0xA802: flag tests, his speech with two PRESS BUTTON pauses, the vote, the next fight's setup cells; no
  REST, clock step or effect clear; *code*, Gold Box Guide 5). The guards' experience and treasure screens come at
  their fight's end, before his speech, so the rings are taken then. **The guards' 12 Rings of Protection +3** come on their pile: TAKE them and READY one on every member in his
  fight: USE, End to the ring, R, EXIT; **its AC counts at once and the member's turn is kept** (*seen*: Combat Chat 38
  in DOSBox, a Ring of Protection +2 readied in a fight, AC 4 to 2; folded by Gold Box Guide 6). **Only the better ring counts**, and
  **magic body armour cancels a ring's AC**; its +3 to saves counts either way (Combat Chat 36: START.EXE 0xE22). So
  every member gains +3 against his fear and breath, and those **without magic body armour** (DORN in the Bracers, the
  magic-users, plain armour) also gain AC 3: Tyranthraxus (THAC0 5) hits AC -1 75% of the time and AC -4 60%. Armour
