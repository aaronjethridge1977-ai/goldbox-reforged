# Secret of the Silver Blades strategy

Secret of the Silver Blades' party rules, route, hard fights and Silver Blades-only rules, for players and for the guide
chats that will work on it. General tactics are in the Gold Box Players' Guide (`docs/playbook.md`); its tags (*seen*,
*code*, *book*, *players*, *Jeremy*, **unconfirmed**) apply here. Made by Gold Box Sources 2 (2026-11-21) from the
game's own documents, following ai-business-ideas `plans/game-sources-method.md`; nothing here has been seen in play
yet. New lessons go in the Inbox at the end; the Lead folds them in daily.

**Sources** (in the GOG folder `Secret of the Silver Blades`; page numbers are the printed ones): the Rule Book (Manual,
pp.1-12: menus only, it sends race and class rules to the Journal), the Data Card (4 pages; its first and last PDF pages also hold the
last page of Curse's card and a Dragonlance Limited Collector's Edition sheet, filed by mistake), the Cluebook (pp.1-60 and a
centre map of the Ruins) and the Adventurer's Journal (pp.1-58: the character rules, bestiary, spells, entries 1-72, no
tavern tales; the tables are the appendices, pp.47-58). The copy protection is a word looked up in the Rule Book or
Journal: its answers stay in `.lab`, as do the Cluebook's riddle answers and passwords (Jeremy, 2026-11-21).

## What changed since Curse

The input for Jeremy's comparison across the series. Party rows first (section 1 has the detail).

9. **A dwarf, a ranger and a thief each have jobs** in Silver Blades' maps (gems, illusions, tracks, traps); a party

10. **Bestow Curse leaves the magic-users' books** on import (*players*); nothing else is known to be taken.

## 2. The route

**The shape** *book* (Cluebook pp.2-4): one valley, in order. Outfit in New Verdigris; through the town's teleport gate to
the Well of Knowledge and kill its Ancient Red Dragon; back in town, confront Marcus the wizard; through the Ruins for
the Amulet of Eldamar and clear the Black Circle's headquarters; down the Mines to Derf at the Temple of Tyr, collect the
eight pieces of Oswulf's staff and have Derf mend it; the malfunctioning teleporter to mine level 9 and the wheel lift
to 10; up the Dungeon (levels 10 to 1) for its three keys; the Western Crevasses (a kidnapped woman), the Frost Giant
Village (win the king over), the Eastern Crevasses; the Castle (Oswulf's spirit lets the staff's bearer in), its traps
and puzzles, the Dreadlord and the gem holding Eldamar's soul.

**What gates what** *book* (Cluebook pp.13-15, 23-24, 43-44, 49)
- **The Ring of Tyr** (Derf, the Temple) shows the staff pieces in the mines and opens Oswulf's tomb on level 9; most
  pieces can't be found without it.
- **The Amulet of Eldamar** (the Ruins' dragon) shows the Dungeon's three keys; without it none can be found.
- **The Staff of Oswulf** (eight pieces, mended by Derf) lets the party past the spirit at the castle gate.
- **The three keys** open the way to the Dreadlord; a wrong key at the Sanctum's doors summons a fight.

**Levels:** none in the books (new characters start at 8, section 1). Levels per stretch come from players and our own
runs: **unconfirmed**.

## 3. Hard fights

From the books only (*book*: the Cluebook unless marked; the monster notes are its "Monsters: How to Deal with Them",
pp.52-58); nothing here has been seen in play. The general rules: buff before a set fight (Bless, Prayer, Haste,
Enlarge, Resist Fire or Cold as the enemy needs); spread the party against breath and area spells; ready a **mirror**
against gazes (basilisk, medusa); Haste ages each member a year (Journal p.24).

**The Well and the town**
- **The Well's red dragons:** eight hatchlings (18) and three sub-adults (19), and the Ancient Red Dragon at the centre.
  Their breath can kill a party quickly: Resist Fire on everyone, spread out, magic-users back and casting; Fireball and
  Ice Storm for the hatchlings (pp.8, 52-53).
- **Marcus's house (11):** a trap takes about 30 HP from each member, then three cockatrices, two medusae and three fire
  giants. Ready mirrors; keep high-AC members away from the cockatrices; no Fireball on the fire giants. The six
  assassins in 12 end the attacks on the party (p.6).

**The Ruins and the Mines**
- **The Ruins' Ancient Red Dragon (13)** guards the Amulet: as the Well's (pp.27-28, 52).
- **The Lizard King (mine 6):** ten giant snakes and ten lizard men; prepare before entering; Fireball and Ice Storm on
  the snakes before they bite. The barracks (63) holds 30 lizard men: Fireball, Ice Storm, Death Spell (p.20).
- **Margoyles and gargoyles (mine 9):** five each guard the force wall's artifact; magic weapons only (pp.21, 53, 56).

**The Dungeon and the Drider Base**
- **Driders** (everywhere below): all cast spells, resist magic and bite with poison. Damage as many as possible each
  round to stop their casting; keep them off the high-AC members (p.53).
- **Purple worms** (Dungeon 2-1): poisonous, hit often, take a lot. Kill at range, or place the party so the nearest
  worm blocks the rest (pp.33-34, 57).
- **Five iron golems (Drider Base 26):** only weapons +3 or better hurt them; Lightning Bolt slows them; **Fireball heals
  them**. Enlarge on members with good weapons; keep the rest away (they are slow); Haste if it goes badly. "A very
  difficult fight" (pp.37, 55).

**The Crevasses and the village**
- **Ten remorhaz (village 17):** one good hit swallows and kills any fighter. All spells at once; keep most of them away
  from the party, as for purple worms (pp.41, 57).
- **Three Ancient Red Dragons (Eastern Crevasses 8, the Black Circle's post):** Bless, Prayer, Haste, Enlarge and Resist
  Fire on as many as possible first (p.43).
- **Giants:** all but storm giants throw boulders: high-AC members out of their sight; a ranger adds a point of damage a
  level; the Sword vs. Giants; no Fireball on fire giants, no cold (Ice Storm) on frost giants (pp.53-54).
- **Mastodons:** surprisingly dangerous; mass damage spells (p.56).

**The Castle**
- **Sargatha** (the castellan, middle level): don't sign or read her scroll (signing sends the party to Dungeon 10, 4;
  reading it starts the fight); attack at once: four basilisks, four storm giants, six medusae (p.45). Met again at 21:
  don't chase her into 22's teleport trap; she sends six storm giants, four cockatrices and four medusae (p.47).
- **Seven storm giants (middle level 9):** their Lightning Bolts bounce off walls and can hit a member twice. Drop
  Fireball and Ice Storm on them even with members inside; every caster able puts up a Minor Globe or Globe of
  Invulnerability (immune to the bolts) (pp.47, 54).
- **The Dreadlord (Sanctum 22), "the most difficult battle in the game":** ten storm giants and six Banite priests.
  Before entering: Enlarge on every fighter, Haste, Bless, Prayer, and Globe of Invulnerability on every magic-user. If
  the enemy wins initiative and casts first, retreat round a corner and let them come; otherwise Fireball and Ice Storm
  to stop the casting while the fighters go for the Dreadlord. Spells and missiles barely touch him; his AC needs the
  best fighters, and a thief's backstab is the best chance once his guards are dead (pp.51, 55).
- **The Vault of the Soul (24):** twelve iron golems and six medusae guard the gem; winning ends the game. Mirrors, +3
  weapons, no Fireball (p.51).

## 4. Silver Blades-only rules

*book* unless marked; nothing here has been seen in play. Where a rule matches Curse's, it isn't repeated.

**The valley and its places**
- **Teleporters link the valley:** the town's gate to the Well, the Well's gates to the Ruins, the headquarters, the
  Temple of Tyr, Dungeon level 0 and the Frost Giant Village; most of the Well's open only once its dragon is dead
  (Cluebook pp.7-8). Fled monsters in the Crevasses pursue until the party passes a teleporter (p.37).
- **The Dungeon's mad spirit** holds the stairs: answer his riddle or fight his iron golems (riddles give more XP); a
  wrong answer sends the party to the Well, which sells the answer for 100 gems (Cluebook pp.8, 23). Answers in `.lab`.
- **Wandering monsters stop** in the Well once its dragon dies; the Dungeon has none until the Dreadlord is dead (only
  patrols); the mines go quiet after the game (Cluebook pp.7, 14, 23).
- **The difficulty level** sets how often rests are interrupted (Crevasses, village, castle) and the XP per fight (Manual
  p.8; Cluebook pp.37-51).

**The mines** (Cluebook pp.14-15)
- **Digging** in worked areas turns up a few gems; a dwarf doubles the find. Bags of 100-200 gems lie about.
- **Toxic fumes:** only a carried canary warns (it dies while the gas is still thin enough to back out); going on does
  serious damage.
- **Umber hulks** burst through walls: dangerous, with good treasure. Looking at one can confuse a member (p.57).
- **Encounters** can be talked to or bribed (about half the time, better with Charisma) before a fight (p.14).

**Money and help**
- **Gems** buy the Well's answers (100 each) and its riddle answers; the Vault keeps money and items and turns 100
  platinum into one gem (Manual p.10; Card p.3; Cluebook p.8).
- **Marcus's magic shop** becomes much cheaper once the townsmen run it (Cluebook p.6).
- **Derf heals** the party free at the Temple of Tyr once the staff is mended (Cluebook pp.12-13).

**Combat** (Journal pp.9-12; Manual pp.11-12)
- **Fleeing:** as Curse, with one exception: a member who reaches the map's edge unseen by every enemy escapes even if
  slower. **If the whole party flees it gets no XP** for the monsters already killed (Journal p.11).
- **Backstab:** from exactly opposite the first attacker; leather or elfin chain only; a better chance to hit and more
  damage. No size limit and no multipliers printed (Curse: man-sized at most; x2 to x4): **unconfirmed**. The Cluebook
  makes it the way to kill the Dreadlord (p.55).
- **A caster hit** can't cast that round and loses a spell already started (Journal p.11).
- **Thieves of 10th level and up** read magic-user scrolls with a 75% chance (Journal p.12).
- **QUICK magic-users never melee** (Manual p.12). Bandage stops a dying member's bleeding (Manual p.12).
- **Monsters' poor weapons and armour** aren't listed among the treasure (Manual p.12).

**Spell notes** (Journal pp.21-26)
- **Fireball** is slow to cast; on the combat screen only the corner squares and the squares directly above and below
  them are safe (Curse's cluebook: three squares in each corner); CENTER first. **Lightning Bolt** is 4 or 8 squares
  long in a line from the caster.
- **Ice Storm** does 3-30 with no save and hurts targets inside a Minor Globe; **Delayed Blast Fireball** also passes a
  Minor Globe; **Minor Globe** stops spells of levels 1-3, **Globe of Invulnerability** levels 1-4.
- **Death Spell** kills outright, more of the weak than of the strong; **Disintegrate** destroys one target (magic
  resistance may save); **Power Word, Stun** stuns one, the weaker the longer; **Mass Invisibility** is Invisibility on
  several.
- **Heal** cures disease, blindness and feeblemind and restores all but 1-4 HP; **Harm** leaves a living creature 1-4 HP;
  only Heal undoes **Feeblemind** (no spells, Intelligence and Wisdom crushed).
- **Barkskin** is AC -1; **Charm Person or Mammal** turns a person or mammal in a fight.
- **Stone to Flesh** (a magic-user spell here) undoes petrification; the temple also cures it (Manual p.4).

## 6. The Journal's false entries

The Journal has entries 1-72 and no tavern tales; some entries are false, and an entry is read when the game names it
(Journal p.27). **The Cluebook lists the true ones** (p.60): 1-7, 12, 13, 15-23, 25, 29-33, 35, 37-41, 43-45, 47, 49-58,
60, 62-71. *book* So these are false, with what each would mislead about:
- **8** (a glowing mouse), **34** (the Flaming One, an old demon, hides in the ruins), **48** (the mouse claims to be the
  Pool's old foe, possessing it) and **59** (a squeaking voice leads the mines' monsters): a red herring about a
  possessing demon. Nothing in the Cluebook's maps supports it.
- **9** (the Well's teleporters curse frequent users into the Black Circle): use them freely.
- **10** (the Temple of Tyr is a demon's trap): the Temple is safe and Derf is an ally (Cluebook pp.12-13).
- **11** and **46** (Mulmaster's Beholder Corps guards the Dungeon): not in the Cluebook's Dungeon.
- **14** (touching the Well's gems withers the hand) and **24** (Grimdra steals the Well's gems).
- **26** (avoid the Amulet of Eldamar; it wakes the Dreadlord): the Amulet is needed for the Dungeon's keys (section 2).
- **27** (the rescued maiden's story) and **28** (the old man's vorpal blade).
- **36** (the map bought from the storm giants): false; the true map is entry 66, on the giants' bodies (Cluebook p.47).
- **42** (the medusa's grudge against the Black Circle) and **61** (the mayor serves Hillsfar and turns on the party).
- **72** (a map to Grimdra's hoard) is in neither list; its subject is entry 24's, which is false: **unconfirmed**.
