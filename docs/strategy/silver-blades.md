# Secret of the Silver Blades strategy

Secret of the Silver Blades' party rules, route, hard fights and Silver Blades-only rules, for players and for the guide
chats that will work on it. General tactics are in the Gold Box Players' Guide (`docs/playbook.md`); its tags (*seen*,
*code*, *book*, *players*, *Jeremy*, **unconfirmed**) apply here. Made by Gold Box Sources 2 (2026-11-21) from the
game's own documents, following ai-business-ideas `plans/game-sources-method.md`; nothing here has been seen in play
yet. New lessons go in the Inbox at the end; the Lead folds them in daily.

**Sources** (in the GOG folder `Secret of the Silver Blades`; page numbers are the printed ones): the Rule Book (Manual,
pp.1-12: menus only, it sends race and class rules to the Journal), the Data Card (4 pages; its first and last PDF pages also hold the
last page of Curse's card and a Dragonlance Limited Collector's Edition sheet, filed by mistake), the Cluebook (pp.1-60 and a
centre map of the Ruins) and the Adventurer's Journal (pp.1-58: the character rules, bestiary, spells, entries 1-72, no
tavern tales; the tables are the appendices, pp.47-58). The copy protection is a word looked up in the Rule Book or
Journal: its answers stay in `.lab`, as do the Cluebook's riddle answers and passwords (Jeremy, 2026-11-21).

## What changed since Curse

The input for Jeremy's comparison across the series. Party rows first (section 1 has the detail).

**Races and classes:** Curse's, unchanged (the six races; paladin and ranger; humans dual-class at a hall). *book*
(Journal pp.3-7)

**Level limits and starting level**
- **Humans to 15 in every class** (Curse: fighter and thief 12, paladin, ranger and magic-user 11, cleric 10);
  **thieves of every race to 18** (Curse 12). Every demi-human limit as in Curse. *book* (Journal p.47)
- **New characters start at level 8** (magic-users 9, thieves 10) with nothing; Curse's started at 25,000 XP (level 5)
  with 300 platinum. *book* (Journal p.5; Manual p.4)
- **The XP bonus at 16** in the prime requisite (Curse's manual: 15). *book* (Journal pp.3-4)

**Importing a Curse party** (section 1): from Curse only; items and money lost (Curse's import from Pool kept 300
platinum); ability gains from readied items kept and Bestow Curse removed, by players. *book* + *players*

**Spells** *book* (Journal pp.21-26, 50-53)
- **New levels:** cleric 6th (Harm, Heal; Wisdom 17+, from cleric 11); magic-user 6th (Death Spell, Disintegrate,
  Flesh to Stone, Globe of Invulnerability, Stone to Flesh; from 12, Intelligence 12+) and 7th (Delayed Blast
  Fireball, Mass Invisibility, Power Word Stun; from 14, Intelligence 14+); the rangers' druid 2nd (Barkskin, Charm
  Person or Mammal, Cure Light Wounds; from ranger 12).
- **Memorizing:** 15 minutes a spell level after 4 hours (levels 1-2) or 6 (3-4), as Curse; the preparation for levels
  5-7 isn't printed (Curse's: 8 hours for 5th): **unconfirmed**. *book* (Journal p.12)
- **FIX** memorizes and casts only Cure Light Wounds, then re-memorizes (Manual p.8); Curse's cluebook said every cure
  spell: **unconfirmed** (request).

**Rules and services**
- **One valley, no overland map:** New Verdigris, the Well of Knowledge, the Ruins, the Mines (10 levels), the Dungeon,
  the Ice Crevasses, the Frost Giant Village and the Castle; the Well's teleporters link them once its dragon is dead.
  *book* (Cluebook pp.1-4, 7-8)
- **Training is free; the temple heals free; identifying costs 100 gp** (Curse: 1000 a level; 200). *book* (Manual
  pp.4, 10; Cluebook p.5)
- **Gems are the currency of help:** the Well answers a question for 100 gems (and the Dungeon's riddles); the Vault
  stores money and items and turns 100 platinum into a gem; the mines give gems to diggers (a dwarf doubles them).
  *book* (Manual p.10; Card p.3; Cluebook pp.5, 8, 15)
- **The armoury sells mirrors** (reflect basilisk and medusa gazes) **and canaries** (warn of the mines' bad air). *book*
  (Cluebook pp.6, 15)
- **Difficulty:** ALTER's LEVEL menu, NOVICE to CHAMPION (VETERAN by default); harder gives more XP per fight, easier
  less, and harder levels interrupt rests more and let fewer flights succeed. Whether Curse had it: **unconfirmed**
  (its books don't say). *book* (Manual p.8; Card p.2; Cluebook pp.14, 38-39)
- **Mine encounters** offer TALK, BRIBE, FLEE and ATTACK; talk and bribe work about half the time, better with
  Charisma. *book* (Cluebook p.14)

**Items** *book* (Journal pp.13-16; Cluebook pp.59-60; section 5 has where)
- **The mayor's starting gifts:** Gauntlets of Ogre Power, bracers, a wand, a scroll, a Mace +1, Leather +1 (Journal
  p.8).
- **New to the series' books:** Silver Long Sword +5 and Silver Shield +5 (the shield reflects gazes like a mirror),
  Boots of Speed, Periapt of Health (no disease), Stone of Good Luck (+1 to most saves), Eyes of Charming (Charm Person,
  never runs out), Long Sword vs. Giants, Wand of Ice Storm, Wand of Paralysis, Elixir of Youth, Girdle of Giant
  Strength; weapons and armour to +5.
- **The Ring of Wizardry** doubles a magic-user's **5th-level** spells (Curse's: levels 1-3). *book* (Cluebook p.60;
  Curse Cluebook p.43) Which in the code: **unconfirmed**.

**Monsters:** new against Curse's list: red dragons (hatchlings, sub-adult, ancient) and white dragons, driders, iron
golems, the lich (the Dreadlord), pyro-hydras and 12- and 16-headed hydras, purple worms, remorhaz, umber hulks, cloud,
frost, fire and hill giants, mastodons, mobats, megalo-centipedes, cockatrices, basilisks, displacer beasts, griffons and
hippogriffs, ettins, lizard men and their king, wyverns, worgs, gyno-sphinxes, giant crocodiles and snakes. Human foes
are named by class and level (a BC Lord is a 13th-level fighter with plate and shield +1, a Legionnaire 14th with +3
gear: Cluebook p.4). *book* (Journal pp.16-20; Cluebook pp.4, 52-58)

**Interface** *book* (Manual pp.6-12; Card pp.1-3)
- **Mouse and joystick** (click the 3D view's edge to move, a target square in combat); ESC takes back a combat move;
  ALT Q, ALT M, Space, CTRL S as Curse's.
- **Scrolls can be joined into bundles** (Card p.3); **PICS** turns the close-up animations off (Manual p.8);
  **DISPLAY** lists the effects on the party (Manual p.9); **AREA** is not available everywhere (Manual p.6).
- **The copy protection** asks for a word from the Rule Book or Journal (Manual p.1; Card p.3). Its answers stay in
  `.lab`.

## 1. The party

**Allowed races and classes** *book* (Journal pp.3-4, 47): Curse's, unchanged.

| Race | Single classes | Multi-classes |
|---|---|---|
| Human | cleric, fighter, magic-user, thief, paladin, ranger; dual-class later | none |
| Half-elf | cleric, fighter, magic-user, thief, ranger | C/F, C/R, C/MU, F/MU, F/T, MU/T, C/F/MU, F/MU/T |
| Elf | fighter, magic-user, thief | F/MU, F/T, MU/T, F/MU/T |
| Dwarf, gnome, halfling | fighter, thief | F/T |

Paladin (lawful good; Str 12, Int 9, Wis 13, Cha 17 at least) and ranger (good; Str 13, Int 13, Wis 14, Con 14 at least)
as in Curse. *book* (Journal pp.6-7)

**Level limits, Silver Blades beside Curse** (the highest level each race can reach in each class; "-" = not allowed;
the band is the class's prime requisite). *book* (Silver Blades Journal p.47; Curse Manual p.23)

| Race | Fighter Str 16- / 17 / 18+ | Ranger Str 16- / 17 / 18+ | Magic-user Int 16- / 17 / 18 | Cleric | Thief | Paladin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dwarf | 7 / 8 / 9 | - | - | - | **18** (Curse 12) | - |
| Elf | 5 / 6 / 7 | - | 9 / 10 / 11 | - | **18** (12) | - |
| Gnome | 5 / 5 / 6 | - | - | - | **18** (12) | - |
| Half-elf | 6 / 7 / 8 | 6 / 7 / 8 | 6 / 7 / 8 | 5 | **18** (12) | - |
| Halfling | 4 / 5 / - (Str 17 at most) | - | - | - | **18** (12) | - |
| Human | **15** (12) | **15** (11) | **15** (11) | **15** (10) | **18** (12) | **15** (11) |

- **Every demi-human limit is Curse's;** only the top of the table moved: every human class to 15, thieves of every race
  to 18 (the XP table goes to 18 for thieves, 15 for the rest: Journal pp.55-58). Whether the hall enforces the table
  as printed: **unconfirmed** (request). *book*
- **A demi-human fighter's limit follows its Strength,** a magic-user's its Intelligence, as in Curse: 18 Strength is
  worth two fighter levels to a dwarf or elf. *book* (Journal pp.3-4, 47)
- **Ability ranges by race and racial modifiers:** the same as Curse's (dwarf Con +1, Cha -1; elf Dex +1, Con -1;
  halfling Dex +1, Str -1; a woman's Strength maximum is lower: human 18/50, dwarf 17). *book* (Journal p.47)
- **The XP bonus** (10%) needs **16** in the prime requisite (paladins Strength and Wisdom, rangers Strength,
  Intelligence and Wisdom, thieves Dexterity). The Journal says 16 throughout; Curse's manual said 15, its cluebook 16.
  *book* (Journal pp.3-4)
- **Spells by level** (Journal pp.55-58): clerics get 6th-level spells at 11, **only with Wisdom 17+**; magic-users 6th
  at 12 (**Intelligence 12+**), 7th at 14 (**Intelligence 14+**); rangers druid spells from 8 (2nd level at 12) and
  magic-user spells from 9 (2nd at 13); paladins cleric spells from 9 (up to 4th level at 15). Wisdom's bonus spells
  reach 4th level at Wisdom 18. *book*
- **Attacks per round:** fighters and paladins 3 every 2 rounds from 7, 2 from 13; rangers 3 every 2 from 8, 2 from 15.
  *book* (Journal pp.10, 49) *players* add that a fighter dual-classed to thief at 13 keeps two a round
  ([GOG forum](https://gog.com/forum/forgotten_realms_collection/party_advice_for_sotsb/page1)).
- **Dual-classing** (humans, at a hall): as in Curse; the old class stops, no new HP and none of the old class's
  abilities until the new level passes the old; a dual-class magic-user can't cast in armour. *book* (Journal p.7;
  Manual p.4)

**New characters start at level 8** (magic-users 9, thieves 10); a demi-human whose limit is lower starts at its limit
and never trains (the Journal's example: a gnome fighter with 18 Strength starts at 6). *book* (Journal p.5) How a
multi-class's start splits (the game's own fighter/thief is fighter 7 / thief 8): **unconfirmed**.

**The game's parties**
- **The Journal's two samples** (p.8): a human paladin, a dwarf fighter/thief, three human rangers and a human cleric; or
  a paladin, a ranger, a dwarf fighter/thief, two magic-users and a cleric (all human but the dwarf). Its advice: one or
  two rangers dual-class to magic-user after 9 (225,001 XP) to cast in armour; a cleric is essential; the paladin's
  protection from evil covers adjacent members. *book*
- **The ready-made party** in the GOG folder (the shipped saved game; read from its six character files, Gold Box
  Sources 2): a human paladin 8 (Str 18/00, Cha 17), a human ranger 8 (18/51), a human fighter 8 (18/50), a human cleric
  8 (Wis 18), a human magic-user 9 (Int 18) and a fighter 7 / thief 8 (the race byte reads as the Journal's dwarf or a
  gnome: **unconfirmed**). *code* (CHRDATA1-6.SAV: class 0x6C, levels 0x111-0x118)
- **Who is useful where** (Cluebook): a **dwarf** doubles the gems dug in the mines (p.15) and spots illusionary walls
  (p.26, Dungeon 18-19) and the mine's trap door (p.16, with thieves); a **ranger** reads the tracks in the Crevasses
  (pp.37-40, 43-44); a **thief** detects and disarms most of the Dungeon's and Castle's traps (pp.25, 31, 45-50); the
  member with the highest **Charisma** talks or bribes in the mines (about 50%) and leads parleys (p.14; Journal p.5).
  No class, race or sex is required by the story (none named, unlike Curse's Swanmays).
- **NPCs:** Vala (a 13th-level fighter, freed on mine level 6 with the Ring of Tyr: "a very good idea" to take her) and
  Sir Deric (the Drider Base only; arrives without gear: arm him or give him a missile weapon) (Cluebook pp.19-20, 35-36).
  Two NPCs at most; a conscious NPC keeps what he is given (Manual p.4).

**Importing a party** *book* (Manual pp.1, 3-4; Card pp.3-4; Cluebook pp.1-2) + *players*
- **From:** Curse of the Azure Bonds only (ADD CHARACTER TO PARTY, FROM WHERE: SECRET or CURSE); Pool's characters come
  through Curse. Remove them from Curse's party, copy Curse's save files into Silver Blades' save folder, then ADD from
  CURSE. *book* (Manual p.3; Card pp.3-4) Gateway to the Savage Frontier's characters also import, as "Curse" characters.
  *players* ([Gold Box wiki, transfer guide](https://goldbox.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Box_Forgotten_Realms_GOG_Transfer_Guide?oldid=4365))
- **Lost:** every item and all money. The story covers it: the Well summons the heroes without gear or money, and the
  mayor gives magic items and money to outfit them (Cluebook p.2; Manual pp.1, 4; Journal p.1). *book* + *players*
  (transfer guide)
- **Kept, by players:** ability changes from items readied in Curse stay after the items are gone; effects carry over
  when the effects file is renamed (some change: Dust of Disappearance's invisibility becomes "magically highlighted");
  **Bestow Curse is taken out of magic-users' books** (it can be learned again). *players* (transfer guide) Levels,
  experience, HP, age and the rest: **unconfirmed** in the code (request).
- **Age:** the Journal warns that humans from Curse, or anyone hasted often, may be near the end of their lifespan
  (Journal p.3). Haste still ages a year a cast (Journal p.24).
- **Train at once:** training is free and New Verdigris' hall (Cluebook p.5) is open from the start; a member with XP
  for two levels trains one and keeps one point short of the next (Manual p.4; Journal p.5).
- **On to Pools of Darkness:** Silver Blades exports to Pools of Darkness, which lowers Strength above 18/00 without a
  girdle to 18/00 and takes back Constitution from Curse's Girdle of the Dwarves. *players* (transfer guide) Players
  dual-class at 15 only when going on to Pools (the old class comes back at 16 there). *players* (GOG forum, above)

**What Silver Blades changes for a party built in Pool or Curse to carry on** (flags, not a recommendation; Jeremy,
2026-11-21: the pick comes once all four games are processed)
1. **Humans pull away:** every human class goes to 15, demi-humans stay at Curse's limits. A demi-human fighter or
   cleric at its limit in Curse (half-elf cleric 5, gnome fighter 6, halfling fighter 5, elf fighter 7, dwarf fighter 9)
   gains nothing more here. *book* (Journal p.47)
2. **A fresh party starts at 8** (magic-users 9, thieves 10): a Curse party below that gains little by import but its
   history; one above it starts ahead. *book* (Journal p.5) How far a finished Curse party usually is: **unconfirmed**
   (players, step 6).
3. **Thieves of every race go to 18:** a demi-human fighter/thief or magic-user/thief keeps growing as a thief after its
   other class stops. *book* (Journal p.47)
4. **Stats for the new spell levels:** a cleric needs **Wisdom 17** for 6th-level spells (Heal, Harm); a magic-user
   **Intelligence 14** for 7th (12 for 6th). Pool's and Curse's scores carry over, so these are set when the character
   is made. *book* (Journal pp.55-56)
5. **XP bonus at 16, not 15:** a prime requisite of 15 costs the 10% here (and in Curse by its cluebook). *book*
6. **Gear doesn't carry, again:** Curse's items stay in Curse. Players report that ability gains from readied items
   (Curse's Gauntlets of Dexterity, the Girdle of Storm Giant Strength, the ioun stone) stay; Pools of Darkness later
   takes Strength above 18/00 back. **unconfirmed** in the code. *players* (transfer guide)
7. **Age:** humans hasted often in Curse arrive older; the Journal flags it. Keep Haste for fights that need it.
   *book* (Journal pp.3, 24)
8. **Elves still can't be raised.** *book* (Journal p.3; Manual p.4)
9. **A dwarf, a ranger and a thief each have jobs** in Silver Blades' maps (gems, illusions, tracks, traps); a party
   without a thief must spring the castle's traps. *book* (Cluebook, above)
10. **Bestow Curse leaves the magic-users' books** on import (*players*); nothing else is known to be taken.
