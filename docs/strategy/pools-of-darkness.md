# Pools of Darkness strategy

Pools of Darkness' party rules, route, hard fights and Pools-only rules, for players and for the guide chats that will
work on it. General tactics are in the Gold Box Players' Guide (`docs/playbook.md`); its tags (*seen*, *code*, *book*,
*players*, *Jeremy*, **unconfirmed**) apply here. Made by Gold Box Sources 3 (2026-11-21) from the game's own
documents, following ai-business-ideas `plans/game-sources-method.md`; nothing here has been seen in play yet. New
lessons go in the Inbox at the end; the Lead folds them in daily.

**Sources** (in the GOG folder `Pools of Darkness`; page numbers are the printed ones): the Rule Book (Manual, pp.1-12:
menus, Limbo and the import; it sends race and class rules to the Journal), the Data Card (six unnumbered panels, cited
as "Card panel n"; panel 6 is a list of program changes), the Cluebook (pp.1-74: synopsis, the Journal's false entries,
the magic items' places, then every area's map notes, and Dave's Challenge) and the Adventurer's Journal (pp.1-60: the
character rules, bestiary, spells, entries, and the tables as appendices, pp.47-59). The Manual, Card and Journal are
scans without text (read by OCR and as images); the Cluebook has a text layer. The copy protection is a word looked up
in the Journal: its answers stay in `.lab`, as do the Cluebook's riddle answers, code words and passwords.

## What changed since Silver Blades

The input for Jeremy's comparison across the series. Party rows first (section 1 has the detail).

**Races and classes:** Silver Blades', unchanged. *book* (Journal pp.1-4, 48)

**Level limits and starting level**
- **Humans and thieves to 40** (Silver Blades: humans 15, thieves 18); every demi-human limit unchanged since Pool. The
  books' advice: demi-humans only as thieves. Whether the hall stops at 40: **unconfirmed**. *book* (Cluebook p.1;
  Journal p.48) + *players*
- **New characters start with 1,500,001 XP** (about level 14: paladin 12 to thief 16); Silver Blades' started at level 8.
  A demi-human starts at its limit. *book* (Journal p.5; Card panel 6)
- **The XP bonus at 16,** as Silver Blades. *book* (Journal p.2)

**Importing a Silver Blades party** (section 1): **the gear and money come too** (Silver Blades' import lost both);
bundled scrolls and the Vault don't. Players: imported characters arrive one XP short of 16. *book* (Card panels 5-6) +
*players*

**Spells** *book* (Journal pp.17-24, 55-59)
- **New levels:** cleric 7th (Destruction, Energy Drain, Resurrection, Restoration; from 16, **Wisdom 18**); magic-user
  8th (Mass Charm, Mind Blank, Otto's Irresistible Dance, Power Word Blind; from 16, **Intelligence 16**) and 9th
  (Meteor Swarm, Monster Summoning, Power Word Kill; from 18, **Intelligence 18**); the rangers' druid 3rd level (Cure
  Disease, Hold Animal, Neutralize Poison, Protection from Fire; from ranger 16).
- **Memorizing:** 15 minutes a spell level after 4 hours (levels 1-2), 6 (3-4), 8 (5-6), 10 (7-8) or 12 (9): the first
  full table in the series' books. *book* (Journal p.9)
- **FIX only heals** now (no memorizing; Silver Blades' FIX re-memorized Cure Light Wounds). *book* (Card panel 6)

**Rules and services**
- **The Pools and Limbo:** a hub (Elminster: storage, healing, training, travel) between the Realms and three outer
  dimensions; **most magic items are destroyed** on the full passage unless stored (section 4). New in the series.
  *book* (Manual p.10; Cluebook p.15)
- **An overland map again** (Silver Blades had none): the Realms, Moander's body and the Web each have one. *book*
- **Temples free** (as Silver Blades); **identifying 100 platinum** (Silver Blades: 100 gold); training free in most
  halls, but Zhentil Keep's costs 10 gems and Mulmaster's a 100-platinum bribe. *book* (Manual p.9; Cluebook pp.31, 63)
- **REST and FIX are refused in some areas.** *book* (Card panel 6)
- **The final fight forbids the party's magic,** and the Palace runs on a clock. *book* (Cluebook pp.71-73)
- **Dave's Challenge:** an optional adventure after the ending, always at Champion, needing every class. New in the
  series. *book* (Cluebook p.73)

**Items** *book* (Cluebook pp.11-12; section 5): **+4 is the common top** (Silver Blades had +5); new to the series'
books: the Vorpal Long Sword, Ring of Gaze Resistance, Ring of Blinking, Ring of Vulnerability (cursed), Javelin of
Lightning, Blessed Bolts, Drow Long Sword and Elfin Chain (they survive the Web), the Fine Long Bow (not magic: it
survives the Pools, *players*).

**Monsters** *book* (Journal pp.15-18, its "New Monsters" list): Bane minions (black, blue, red), Pets of Kalistes,
gaze, stone, electric and enormous spiders, giant cockatrices, greater margoyles, greater otyughs, large iron golems,
walking trees, hill giant shamans; and the four lieutenants (Thorne, an ancient red
dragon; Kalistes, a marilith; Tanetal, a glabrezu; Gothmenes, a balor).

**Interface** *book* (Manual pp.3-12; Card panels 4-6): as Silver Blades' (mouse, joystick, ESC takes back a move,
DISPLAY, AREA not everywhere); **a purple name** marks a character ready to train. Players: targeting skips the
party's own members and spells cast are re-memorized on REST. *players*
([CRPG Addict](https://crpgaddict.blogspot.com/2016/09/game-230-pools-of-darkness-1991.html))

## 1. The party

**Allowed races and classes** *book* (Journal pp.1-2, 48): Silver Blades', unchanged.

| Race | Single classes | Multi-classes |
|---|---|---|
| Human | cleric, fighter, magic-user, thief, paladin, ranger; dual-class later | none |
| Half-elf | cleric, fighter, magic-user, thief, ranger | C/F, C/R, C/MU, F/MU, F/T, MU/T, C/F/MU, F/MU/T |
| Elf | fighter, magic-user, thief | F/MU, F/T, MU/T, F/MU/T |
| Dwarf, gnome, halfling | fighter, thief | F/T |

Paladin (lawful good; Str 12, Int 9, Wis 13, Cha 17 at least) and ranger (good; Str 13, Int 13, Wis 14, Con 14 at
least) as before. *book* (Journal p.3)

**Level limits across the series** (the highest level each race can reach in each class; "-" = not allowed; the band is
the class's prime requisite). Pools' row printed first, the earlier games in brackets. *book* (Pools Journal p.48;
Silver Blades Journal p.47; Curse Manual p.23) + *code* for Curse and Pool (curse.md section 1)

| Race | Fighter Str 16- / 17 / 18+ | Ranger Str 16- / 17 / 18+ | Magic-user Int 16- / 17 / 18 | Cleric | Thief | Paladin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dwarf | 7 / 8 / 9 | - | - | - | **40** (SB 18, Curse 12) | - |
| Elf | 5 / 6 / 7 | - | 9 / 10 / 11 | - | **40** (18, 12) | - |
| Gnome | 5 / 5 / 6 | - | - | - | **40** (18, 12) | - |
| Half-elf | 6 / 7 / 8 | 6 / 7 / 8 | 6 / 7 / 8 | 5 | **40** (18, 12) | - |
| Halfling | 4 / 5 / 6 (Str 17 at most) | - | - | - | **40** (18, 12) | - |
| Human | **40** (15, 12) | **40** (15, 11) | **40** (15, 11) | **40** (15, 10) | **40** (18, 12) | **40** (15, 11) |

- **Every demi-human limit is the same in all four games;** only humans and thieves move. In Pool the halls stopped
  every class early (fighter 8, thief 9, cleric and magic-user 6), so few demi-human limits bit there. *book* + *code*
- **40 is the Cluebook's number** ("all characters ... should be able to advance up to level 40", p.1). The Journal's
  table only says "max" for humans and thieves, "the highest level available in Pools of Darkness" (p.48), and its XP
  tables print levels 1-18, then a fixed cost per level after 18th (pp.56-59: fighter 250,000, paladin 350,000, ranger
  325,000, cleric 225,000, magic-user 375,000, thief 220,000). Whether the hall stops at 40, and for which classes:
  **unconfirmed** (request). Players agree on 40. *players* ([GameBanshee](https://gamebanshee.com/poolsofdarkness/walkthrough/gameplaynotes.php);
  [GOG forum](https://gog.com/forum/forgotten_realms_collection/should_i_transfer_characters_goldbox_games))
- **The halfling's 6 at Str 18+** is printed here (Silver Blades printed "-"), but a halfling can't have more than 17
  Strength (Journal p.48), so it changes nothing. Pool's code also had the 6. *book*
- **Ability ranges and racial modifiers:** the same as before (dwarf Con +1, Cha -1; elf Dex +1, Con -1; halfling Dex +1,
  Str -1; a woman's Strength maximum is lower: human 18/50, dwarf 17). *book* (Journal pp.47-48)
- **The XP bonus** needs **16** in the prime requisite, as in Silver Blades. *book* (Journal p.2)
- **Spells need high scores.** Magic-users: 5th level at 9 (Int 10+), 6th at 12 (Int 12+), 7th at 14 (Int 14+), **8th at
  16 (Int 16+), 9th at 18 (Int 18+)**: Mass Charm, Mind Blank, Otto's Irresistible Dance, Power Word Blind; Meteor
  Swarm, Monster Summoning, Power Word Kill. Clerics: 6th level at 11 (Wis 17+), **7th at 16 (Wis 18+)**: Destruction,
  Energy Drain, Resurrection, Restoration. Rangers: druid 3rd level at 16. Paladins: cleric spells from 9, 4th level at
  15. *book* (Journal pp.17-24, 55-59) The Card and Cluebook both say a magic-user needs **18 Intelligence** or the most
  powerful spells are out of reach. *book* (Card panel 6; Cluebook p.1)
- **Attacks per round:** as before (fighters and paladins 3 every 2 rounds from 7, 2 from 13; rangers 3 every 2 from 8,
  2 from 15). *book* (Journal p.49)
- **Dual-classing** (humans, at a hall): as before; the old class stops, no new HP and none of its abilities until the
  new level passes the old. A dual-class magic-user can't cast in armour, **except a ranger/magic-user** (the Journal's
  wording; Silver Blades' Journal had no exception): **unconfirmed** (request). *book* (Journal p.4)

**New characters start with 1,500,001 XP** ("most single-class characters at about 14th level"). By the tables that is
paladin 12, ranger 13, fighter, cleric and magic-user 14, thief 16; a two-class character gets 750,000 in each class, a
three-class 500,000 (our arithmetic). A demi-human whose limit is lower starts at its limit: the Card's warning that
"most non-human races" start at their class maximums (panel 6). *book* (Journal p.5; Journal pp.56-59; Card panel 6) The
books don't say what new characters carry; players say about level 14 with +2 and +3 equipment. *players* ([GameBanshee](https://gamebanshee.com/poolsofdarkness/walkthrough/gameplaynotes.php))

**Non-humans: thieves only.** The Cluebook's advice (p.1): use demi-humans only as thieves, the one class whose
advancement is unlimited for them, and give magic-users 18 Intelligence; a party with demi-humans in other classes, or a
magic-user under 18, should lower the difficulty to Novice or Squire (ALTER, LEVEL), because the game was built for a
party without those limits. Players say the same. *book* + *players* (GameBanshee, above)

**The game's parties**
- **The Journal's sample** (p.6): a human magic-user, a human paladin, a dwarf fighter/thief, a human ranger and two
  human clerics. Its reasons: mostly single-class, so it advances quickly; the paladin's protection from evil (AC 2
  better against evil foes for everyone within 10 feet); the fighter/thief's locks and traps with a fighter's armour and
  HP; the ranger's damage against giants and his spells; clerics "absolutely essential" for healing. *book*
- **The ready-made party** (Save Game A, outfitted, "at the very beginning of the game": Card panel 1; read from its six
  character files, Gold Box Sources 3): a human paladin 12 (Str 18/00, Wis 18, Cha 17), a human ranger 13 (18/50), a
  human cleric 14 (Wis 18), two human magic-users at 12 (Int 18) and 13 (Int 17, Str 18/51: perhaps a dual-classed
  fighter), and a fighter 8 / thief 13 (the race byte reads as dwarf). The magic-users' and the fighter/thief's levels
  don't match the XP tables at 1,500,001 (14; fighter 9 at most for a dwarf, thief 14): **unconfirmed**. *code*
  (CHRDATA1-6.SAV: race 0xAD, class 0xAE, levels 0x151-0x158)
- **Who is useful where** (Cluebook): a **high-level thief** steals the antidote vial in Kalistes' Temple (p.39); an
  **elf** recognizes the Watcher's haunts in Moander's heart (p.60); a member with **Strength 15** (or a Strength or
  Enlarge spell) passes the Testing Ground's first trial (p.45); the **highest Dexterity** crosses the slave pens' spike
  pit (p.37); a **strong member** forces the Descent's stuck door wide enough for a **halfling, gnome or elf** (p.35);
  high Dexterity or a thief's climbing gets over Myth Drannor's wall (p.51). **Dave's Challenge** (optional, after the
  ending, always at Champion) needs at least one magic-user, thief, cleric and fighter-type (p.73).
- **NPCs** (two at most; a conscious NPC keeps what he is given: Manual p.4): Vala (Vala vs. Vaasa; gives a silver
  shield +4), Raizel (a thief, Thorne's Cave: leads the party astray to treasure), Traned (a halfling, the Descent: her
  silver shield reflects the Manshoon sentinel's gaze), Silk (a drow, the slave pens), Storm (a dwarf ranger, the Web
  Dimension: "fight steadfastly"), Dennia or Valardis (the Web; Valardis betrays), Shal (Tower of Marcus: a fighter
  without her magic; arm her), Nacacia (Myth Drannor), and two who join to betray: Ruoln (Kimarr in disguise, Thar) and
  the disgruntled slave (the Web). *book* (Cluebook pp.17-56)

**Importing a party** *book* (Manual pp.1, 3; Card panels 2-3, 5-6) + *players*
- **From:** Secret of the Silver Blades only (Pool and Curse characters come through it). Two ways: LOAD SAVED GAME,
  WHERE FROM SECRET, takes a whole saved party (the preferred way); ADD CHARACTER TO PARTY, SECRET, takes one character
  removed from Silver Blades' party. Setup asks for Silver Blades' save path (default `SECRET\SAVE`). *book* On GOG:
  make a `SECRET` folder in the Pools of Darkness folder and copy Silver Blades' `SAVE` folder into it. *players*
  ([Gold Box wiki, transfer guide](https://goldbox.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Box_Forgotten_Realms_GOG_Transfer_Guide?oldid=4365);
  [GOG forum](https://gog.com/forum/forgotten_realms_collection/sotsb_to_por_transfer))
- **Kept:** the characters, "most of their equipment" and their money: the first import since Pool's that keeps gear.
  *book* (Card panel 5) Players: all gear and money came across. *players*
  ([CRPG Addict](https://crpgaddict.blogspot.com/2016/09/game-230-pools-of-darkness-1991.html))
- **Lost:** **bundled scrolls** (unbundle them in Silver Blades first) and **the Vault** (give its items to the
  characters first). *book* (Card panel 6) Strength above 18/00 without a girdle comes down to 18/00, and Constitution
  from Curse's Girdle of the Dwarves goes; stat-changing items can confuse the transfer. *players* (transfer guide;
  [GOG forum](https://gog.com/forum/forgotten_realms_collection/should_i_transfer_characters_goldbox_games))
- **Levels and XP:** players report imported characters arriving one XP short of level 16. *players* (GameBanshee) The
  books don't say; what the code copies (HP, XP, spells, effects, age): **unconfirmed** (request).
- **Training at once:** the training hall is in Phlan (east side), but Phlan is torn away early in the story and only
  comes back after Marcus' Tower; players say training is out of reach for a while after the start. Train in Phlan
  before escorting Sasha. *book* (Cluebook pp.1, 13) + *players* (GOG forum, above)
- **Gear in the Pools:** most magic items are destroyed on the full passage between the Realms and an outer dimension
  (either way); store them with Elminster in Limbo first. Going from an outer dimension to Limbo and back keeps them;
  drow gear survives into the Web Dimension, and Shal's vorpal long sword survives Limbo. An imported party's Silver
  Blades gear (+5 weapons and armour; Pools' is mostly +4) is the reason to import even demi-humans: players import for
  the gear, then make new humans where needed. *book* (Manual p.10; Cluebook pp.15, 40, 56) + *players* (GameBanshee)

**What Pools of Darkness changes for a party built in Pool, Curse or Silver Blades** (flags, not a recommendation;
Jeremy, 2026-11-21: the pick comes once all four games are processed)
1. **Humans go to 40, demi-humans stay put.** A demi-human fighter, ranger, magic-user or cleric reaches its limit
   (5-11) by Curse and gains nothing in Silver Blades or Pools; the game's own books say to use non-humans only as
   thieves. A demi-human multi-class has one class still growing only if it is a thief. *book* (Journal p.48; Cluebook p.1)
2. **Magic-users need Intelligence 18** for 9th-level spells, and the books say so twice. Scores are set when the
   character is made in Pool, so this is decided there. *book* (Journal p.56; Card panel 6; Cluebook p.1)
3. **Clerics need Wisdom 18** for 7th-level spells (Resurrection, Restoration), 17 for 6th (Heal). *book* (Journal p.57)
4. **The import keeps the gear,** unlike Pool's and Curse's and Silver Blades' own: what the party carries out of Silver
   Blades is its starting kit (Silver Blades' +5 gear beats most of Pools'). Unbundle scrolls and empty the Vault first.
   *book* (Card panels 5-6) + *players*
5. **A fresh party starts at about 14** (1,500,001 XP); an imported one is reported at 15-16. A carried party's lead is
   small in levels, large in gear. *book* + *players*
6. **Dual-class at 15 in Silver Blades, cash in at 16 in Pools:** players dual-class a human at 15 late in Silver Blades
   so the old class comes back at 16, early in Pools. *players* (GOG forum, above)
7. **An elf and a dwarf still have small jobs** (the Watcher; Storm is an NPC dwarf), and a halfling, gnome or elf one
   door; none is required. A **thief** has the antidote and Dave's Challenge. *book* (Cluebook, above)
8. **Elves still can't be raised;** each raise costs a point of Constitution. *book* (Journal pp.1, 3; Manual p.5)
9. **Age:** Haste ages a character a year; humans have the shortest lives. *book* (Journal p.2)

## 2. The route

**The shape** *book* (Cluebook pp.1-10): start in Phlan, then east; once Vala is helped, west, going round the
overland map roughly counter-clockwise; do the small "mini" adventures as they turn up, not in the synopsis' order.
Phlan is torn from the Realms early (Bane hides it under Marcus' Tower) and Elminster takes the party into **Limbo**,
the hub between the Pools of Darkness: from there the Realms overland, then three outer dimensions (Moander, the Web
Dimension, Bane's Dark Dimension) are reached through the Pools.

**What gates what** *book* (Cluebook pages as marked)
- **The Horn of Doom** (Thorne, after his second blast, before the third: p.29) passes Manshoon's questioning (p.32),
  summons a sentinel's dragons in the Cave of the Beholder (p.67) and cracks Dark Phlan's Iron Wall: blow it once for
  each party member (p.70).
- **The Crystal Ring** (Kalistes, the Web Dimension: pp.46-48) is needed in the Palace of Gothmenes to raise Bane's
  Crystal from the Pool of Death; without it Gothmenes never appears (pp.71-72).
- **The Talisman of Bane** (Tanetal, Moander's heart: pp.60-63) opens the last sentinel hall's fist to the Pool of
  Darkness and Dark Phlan (p.67).
- **The four Aerie keys** (one per dragon nest) open the doors to the dracolich and the gate to Thorne's Cave (pp.27-28).
- **Aeghwaet's two potions** (the Moander mini-adventures, for clearing his wound) are required before entering the
  heart (pp.58-60). **The Ward of Safe Passage** (the Testing Ground) or the slave's secret way gets into Kalistes'
  Parlor; otherwise the party arrives bound in the larder (pp.43, 47).
- **Phlan** can't be entered until Marcus' Tower falls (p.13); the Tower of Marcus rejoins the party to its city.

**Levels:** none in the books (new characters start at about 14, imported ones at 15-16: section 1). Players give no
levels per stretch either; one walkthrough says most characters pass level 36 by the end. *players*
([the Spoiler Centre](https://the-spoiler.com/RPG/SSI/pools.of.darkness.1.html)) Levels per stretch: **unconfirmed**, from our
own runs.

**Notes by stretch** (*book*, the Cluebook unless marked; hard fights in section 3; riddle answers, code words and
passwords in `.lab`)
1. **Phlan** (pp.13-14): temples and the training hall on the east side, the shops on the west (Ohlo's for magic,
   Smithy's armour, the Bowyer, the Old Guild Armory); the fortune teller (16) gives hints all game; the library's old
   man is Elminster. Train before leaving with Sasha (section 1).
2. **Limbo and the minis** (pp.15-17): store magic items with Elminster before passing to the Realms or another
   dimension (section 4). The huntsman's answer is PHLAN or NO ONE; the farmhouse's bodies (1) spare a surprise; save
   the crossroad's storekeeper for his +3 gear and potions of speed.
3. **Thar** (pp.18-19): Ruoln is Kimarr; Bostell (9) helps in the last fight; please Sasha at 20.
4. **Vala** (p.20): tell the hill giants you are there for the "Silver Lady"; take Vala along or the giants fight.
5. **Tyr** (pp.21-22): chase the guard at once; erase the hand after the vampire priest (a blessing); leave the plunder
   at 9 (the curse); don't kill Laurellin or Brimwulf; the ritual's third option (Journal entry 11) means fewer
   elementals.
6. **The Steading** (pp.23-24): bluff in as mercenaries; train free at 6; Kardal's tip (beat the cloud giants at 10)
   opens the inner steading; buy or sell at the store (23) or lose money. **The Fire Giant Cave** (pp.25-26): never
   wear Ungleow's ring; throw the skull's ring down the endless corridor.
7. **The Aerie** (pp.27-28): a key in each nest; burning the dragon bodies (14) stops a new dracolich but draws dragons.
8. **Thorne** (pp.29-30): free the prisoners, take Raizel, and refuse her detours (go north) until Thorne is dead.
9. **Zhentil Keep and Manshoon** (pp.31-33): grovel or bluff at the gate; training costs 10 gems; Mabril's secret way
   (4) skips the questioning; the taxation takes 10 gems a head (3 will do); bring Traned up for the sentinel.
10. **The Descent, the pens, the temple** (pp.34-39): find Traned (6) and let her go with the slaves at the Winnowing;
    LOOK in the barracks (8); help Silk (her amulet, a safe pass, Drow weapons); the temple's secret door from the pens
    is the safe entry; a thief steals the antidote; destroy the teleporter statue's hands before the portal.
11. **The Web** (pp.40-48): befriend Cynthia first (spiders from the fireflies' cavern, Drow prisoners; then kill her
    as she asks); take Storm and Dennia; save often. The Parlor: destroy the eggs to draw Kalistes out.
12. **Myth Drannor; Marcus** (pp.49-56): ready mirrors or silver shields; blessed bolts for the rakshasas; leave the
    elf's grave (XP) or rob it (the Girdle). Rescue Shal from the dungeon and keep her alive (the vorpal sword; she
    reunites with Petra); the Pincer's trick (16-17).
13. **Moander** (pp.57-62): rescue the priestess (the amphora slows the heart); clear Aeghwaet's wound (his potions,

## 3. Hard fights

From the books only (*book*: the Cluebook unless marked; the monster notes from the Journal's bestiary, pp.13-18);
nothing here has been seen in play. The general rules as before: buff before set fights, spread out against breath,
ready **mirrors or silver shields** against gazes, missiles and spells against what melee can't touch.

**The new monsters** (Journal pp.15-17)
- **Bane minions** (Gothmenes' servants: Dark Phlan, the Palace, the Cave of the Beholder): all see invisible things and
  ignore fear, poison, death magic and charm. **Black** breathe acid and cast cold, hold and slow spells; **blue** breathe
  lightning and **return double the damage to anyone striking them** (the Cluebook: don't melee them, use magic and
  missiles, p.65); **red** breathe fire and add 2d6 fire to each hit.
- **Pets of Kalistes:** AC -1, 85% magic resistance, a poison bite saved at -2 or death, Death Spell and Disintegrate
  once a day, blink. **Enormous spiders:** save or die. **Gaze spiders** paralyze (mirrors reflect), **stone spiders**
  petrify, **electric spiders** breathe lightning.
- **Large iron golems** (27 HD, poison breath; only electricity slows them, fire heals them), **greater margoyles**
  (normal weapons useless), **giant cockatrices** (petrify), **walking trees**, **greater otyughs**, hill giant shamans.
- **Rakshasas** (Myth Drannor): spells under 8th level don't affect them; ordinary ones need magic weapons and take half
  from +1 and +2; stronger ones ignore +1 and take half from +2 and +3; **blessed bolts kill them**; Detect Magic shows
  the disguised ones (pp.49-50).

**The Realms**
- **Phlan's gate (W23):** nine Black Mages and a Bit o' Moander hold a family; the gates stay shut until they fall
  (p.14).
- **Kimarr** (Thar) ends as a black dragon in his chambers, after his monsters at 15 (p.19).

## 4. Pools-only rules

*book* unless marked; nothing here has been seen in play. Where a rule matches Silver Blades', it isn't repeated.

**Limbo and the Pools**
- **Limbo's menu:** REPAIR (temple healing), TRAIN, STORAGE, ENCAMP, MOVE ON (Elminster's list of destinations)
  (Manual p.10).
- **Most magic items are destroyed** on the full passage between the Realms and an outer dimension, either way; going
  from an outer dimension into Limbo and back keeps them. Store items and money with Elminster (STORAGE: DEPOSIT, TAKE,
  POOL, MONEY) (Manual p.10; Cluebook p.15). Drow gear passes into the Web Dimension (p.39); Shal's vorpal sword survives
  Limbo (p.56). Players: very few magic items survive; the elemental-resistance rings (rings without a plus) and the
  vorpal sword do; ordinary items all survive, so carry fine long bows and plain arrows (a fine bow adds Strength damage).
  Test by saving before a passage and looking. *players* ([GOG forum](https://gog.com/forum/forgotten_realms_collection/questions_about_items_in_pools_of_darkness);
  [GOG forum](https://gog.com/forum/forgotten_realms_collection/question_about_limbo_and_magical_items_pod)) Which items
  survive, exactly: **unconfirmed** (request).
- **Overland maps:** the Realms, Moander's body and the Web Dimension each have an overland of mini-adventures (pp.15,
  40, 57).

**Services**
- **Temples are free;** Phlan's inns are free (the party's fame); identifying costs **100 platinum** (Manual p.9;
  Cluebook p.13).
- **Training:** Phlan, Taydome's Keep, the Steading (free), Zhentil Keep (10 gems), Moander's hermit, Mulmaster (a
  100-platinum bribe), and Limbo's TRAIN (Cluebook pp.13, 19, 23, 31, 57, 63; Manual p.10). A **purple name** means the
  character can train (Card panel 6).
- **Tolls in gems:** Zhentil Keep's training hall and its mace +4 (10 gems), Manshoon's taxation (pp.31-32).

**Camp and difficulty**
- **FIX only heals** (no time for memorizing: use REST); **REST and FIX are missing in some areas** (Card panel 6).
- **ALTER, LEVEL** as Silver Blades (Novice to Champion; Veteran by default; harder gives more XP) (Manual p.7). Players: the
  level changes the enemies' HP and damage, not saving throws (an easier level won't stop a beholder's disintegration).
  *players* ([GameBanshee](https://gamebanshee.com/poolsofdarkness/walkthrough/gameplaynotes.php)) The
  Cluebook tells parties with demi-humans or a magic-user under Int 18 to play Novice or Squire (p.1). **Dave's
  Challenge** is always Champion (p.73).

**Area rules** (the Cluebook)
- **The Temple of Tyr:** erasing the black hand gives -2 AC and +1 to hit inside the temple; taking the marauders'
  plunder curses the party (+2 AC, -2 to hit there) (pp.21-22).
- **Moander's heart:** LUB flushes a party standing in a ventricle out of the heart, DUB drains one in an atrium into a
  ventricle; fights in the pumping system end with the next one; the gold cornucopia weighs the whole party down;
  resting is dangerous (Tanetal kills sleepers) (pp.60-63).
- **The Cave of the Beholder:** the Chancellor's errands send the party back to the overland, and each one finished
  thins his army (pp.65-66); three sentinel halls summon monsters for each Object of Power carried (the Horn, the
  Ring, the Talisman) (p.67).
- **Dark Phlan's Iron Wall:** blow the Horn once per party member (p.70).
- **The Palace of Gothmenes:** a ticking clock (the Tantalus Clock) after the inner doors; no rest in the inner palace;
  the Objects of Power are handed out at the secret doors (LOOK) and change hands there; reaching into the Pool of
  Death costs terrible damage; a word repeated in the finale is a lethal trap (in `.lab`); the party can't cast
  against Gothmenes (pp.71-73).
- **The ending** turns time back: nobody in the Realms remembers; a ship of treasure waits; the weapons stored in Limbo
  come back at the docks (pp.10, 13).

**Interface** (Manual pp.5-12; Card panels 4-6) as Silver Blades': mouse and joystick, ESC takes back a combat move,
ALT Q, ALT M, Space, CTRL S; DISPLAY shows the effects on the party; AREA isn't available everywhere.

## 6. The Journal's false entries

The Journal has entries 1-100 (pp.25-46), mostly tavern tales, overheard talk and maps; an entry is read when the game
names it, and the Manual warns that some of the tales are fiction (p.1).
**The Cluebook names the false ones** (p.11): **1, 6, 20, 23, 50, 75, 87 and 98**. *book* What each would mislead about:
- **6, 50, 75, 87, 98: the Red Rider and the Flaming Sword.** A tavern tale of a rider with a flaming sword (6), "Bane"
  boasting of a red rider sent to the Realms (50), the sword's legend (75), a skull urging the party to seek it (87) and a
  map to the rider's lair (98): one false quest. Nothing in the Cluebook's areas has it.
- **1:** the magical gates "steal your strength": false; the Pools are the way through (the real cost is item loss in
  passage, section 4).
- **20:** a bard's tale of Auril the Frostmaiden walking the lands.
- **23:** a map of the Lost Mines.

**Entries the Cluebook points to** (by number only; their contents stay in the Journal): **5** (the priest's map of the
slave pens' arches and teleporter floor: pp.36-37), **11** (the ritual's options at the Temple of Tyr: p.22), **24** and
**46** (the Banites' chant: the Cave of the Beholder's labyrinth: pp.64, 68), **74** (Laurellin and Brimwulf: p.22).
