# The series party

One party made in Pool of Radiance and carried through Curse of the Azure Bonds, Secret of the Silver Blades and Pools
of Darkness. **Chosen by Jeremy on 2026-11-22 (Gold Box Series Party 1), for a curiosity run:** carrying a party through
the series is not a project goal (playbook section 1: "importing a finished party is a feature to test"). Tags as in
the playbook (*seen*, *code*, *book*, *players*, *Jeremy*, **unconfirmed**). The games' own rules are in each game's
strategy file, section 1 and "What changed since ..."; this file only draws on them. New lessons go in the Inbox at the
end; the Lead folds them in daily.

## 2. Why all human

- **Every demi-human limit is the same in all four games;** only humans and thieves move up (fighter 8 in Pool's halls,
  12 in Curse, 15 in Silver Blades, 40 in Pools by the Cluebook). Pools' own Cluebook says to use demi-humans only as
  thieves. *book* + *code* (pools-of-darkness.md section 1)
- **A multi-class splits its XP even when one class is capped:** Pool's code halves the share whatever the levels, so
  the capped half is lost (a dwarf fighter/thief's thief side would grow at half speed). *code* (Pool GAME.OVR
  0x58D3-0x5A26, main chat 96) Curse, Silver Blades, Pools: **unconfirmed** (request open).
- **The top spells need scores set in Pool:** cleric 6th Wis 17 and 7th Wis 18; magic-user 7th Int 14, 8th Int 16, 9th
  Int 18 (Silver Blades Journal pp.55-56; Pools Journal pp.55-59). *book*
- **The tabletop:** these are 1st edition rules, where a demi-human's one unlimited class is thief; 2nd edition capped
  every demi-human class (thieves at 12-15), and the "favored class" with no caps is 3rd edition's. *players* (Gold Box
  Series Party 1, 2026-11-21)

## 3. The paladin

- **Pool has no paladins and no class changes;** Curse's training halls offer "Human Change Class": the old class stops,
  the new starts at level 1 with 0 XP on its own table, and the character has only the new class's HP and abilities
  until its level passes the old, then both. *book* (Curse Manual pp.6-8)
- **Whether a Pool fighter can become a paladin, and with which scores: unconfirmed** (the Player's test on the board).
  Players give 15+ in the old class's prime requisite and 17+ in the new; the paladin also needs Str 12, Int 9, Wis 13,
  Cha 17 and lawful good. *players* + *book*
- **When:** not at the start (Tilverton's hall closes after the coach) and not inside a bond dungeon (little rest, no
  hall, and Curse trains one level at a time: XP for several levels is lost). Best right after the first middle bond
  (Hap), when the towns' Search Area opens the side dungeons for XP between visits to a hall; or at the sewers' hidden
  hall if the party can earn there. *players* ([GOG forum](https://www.gog.com/forum/forgotten_realms_collection/new_to_gold_box_games_question_about_party_creation)) + *book* (curse.md sections 2, 4)
- **The cost grows with each fighter level kept** (the paladin must pass it; 1st edition's paladin table, the games'
  own tables not checked against it): from fighter 7, paladin 8 at 175,001 XP; from 8, paladin 9 at 350,001; from 9,
  paladin 10 at 700,001. A paladin fights on the fighter's table, so the change costs HP (none new until he passes),
  not blows. *book* (tabletop)
- **Estimate:** the fighter leaves Pool at 7-8 (Pool's hall stops at 8, 125,001 XP; our Player's ARN had 72,442 near the
  finale) and is about 8, perhaps 9, after Hap. Changing at 8, he likely passes it in Silver Blades. **unconfirmed**
  (Curse's XP per stretch isn't in our files; whether XP over Pool's cap carries is a board request).
- **Fallback if the test says no:** make a fresh paladin in Curse (25,000 XP, level 5) and retire fighter 1.

## 4. What each game costs or gives this party

- **Pool:** six single-class humans, two bows (the fighters), four in the line (clerics fight in it), no thief, harder
  fights from high scores. Halls stop at fighter 8, cleric and magic-user 6.
- **Curse:** gear and money lost (300 platinum each); train at once in Tilverton; the paladin change after Hap; caps
  fighter 12, paladin and magic-user 11, cleric 10. *book* + *players* (curse.md section 1)
- **Silver Blades:** gear and money lost again (the mayor outfits the party); every human class to 15; fresh characters
  start at 8, so a carried party gains little in levels; traps without a thief (request open). *book*
- **Pools:** the import keeps gear and money (unbundle scrolls and empty the Vault first); humans to 40; fresh
  characters start about 14, an imported party is reported one XP short of 16; Silver Blades' +5 gear beats most of
  Pools' +4. The paladin resists Dave's Challenge's test at 16 and keeps his spells longer there. *book* + *players*
- **The story:** one villain (Tyranthraxus) and one home (Phlan) across the four games; each later game explains the
  lost gear, and Pools lets it come along. *players*
